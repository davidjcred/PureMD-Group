# Healthcare Revenue Cycle and Lien Recovery Terms

A simple reference guide to common terms used in California medical billing, personal injury collections, and workers' compensation claims.

### A/R (Accounts Receivable)
Unpaid financial balances owed to a medical practice by patients, commercial insurance companies, or third-party liable entities.

### CAQH (Council for Affordable Quality Healthcare)
An online database where medical providers maintain credentialing details, work history, and license information for insurance panel enrollment.

### DWC (Division of Workers' Compensation)
The California state agency that monitors workers' compensation claims, enforces administrative rules, and oversees the WCAB court system.

### EOR (Explanation of Review)
A document sent by a workers' compensation payer explaining how a medical bill was paid, reduced, or denied. Similar to an EOB in commercial insurance.

### LOP (Letter of Protection)
A legal agreement between a patient, an attorney, and a healthcare provider. It guarantees that the provider will be paid out of any future personal injury settlement or court judgment.

### OMFS (Official Medical Fee Schedule)
The state-mandated fee schedule set by California Labor Code Section 5307.1. It dictates maximum allowable payment rates for medical services rendered to injured workers.

### RFA (Request for Authorization)
The formal document a physician submits to a workers' comp carrier to request approval for medical treatment, surgery, or therapy before rendering care.

### Utilization Review (UR)
The process insurance carriers use to review medical treatment requests against state guidelines to decide if the care is medically necessary.
